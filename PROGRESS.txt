2026-07-16 · Redesign themes — pass 6b: the REAL terminal-contrast fix
TASK: Yossi re-tested — Claude Code's bullet was STILL pale on light
  ground. Root cause understood properly this time: the bullet is drawn
  with 256-colour/truecolor escapes, not the 16 ANSI slots — a theme
  palette can't remap those, and apps pick them assuming a dark ground.
FIX: xterm's built-in contrast floor — `minimumContrastRatio: 4.5`
  (WCAG AA, VS Code's terminal default). The renderer auto-adjusts any
  foreground that misses the ratio against the current background, which
  covers indexed + truecolor output on BOTH light and dark grounds.
  Set at construction + re-asserted in setTerminalTheme (HMR-safe).
FILES:
  M app/src/terminalInstance.ts
RESULT: verified-live on Yossi's active Claude session (Classical light,
  tmux reattach after webview reload): the bullet + dim-gray status lines
  render dark and readable. tsc clean.
NOTE: pass-6 daylight() ANSI ramp stays — it fixes the 16-slot layer
  properly; the contrast floor catches everything the palette can't.
